The icon data is released under CC0-1.0, but that does not transfer any rights in the logos themselves: each logo is a trademark of its respective owner and is shown only to identify the technology used. Where Simple Icons lists brand guidelines or a licence for an icon, they are linked below. Logos without a match in Simple Icons (java, csharp, powershell, pyinstaller, windows, minecraft, fabric) are not included.
